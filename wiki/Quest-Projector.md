# Quest Projector

Place and right-click the projector to configure quests, images, formatted text and appearance. Only the placer or an operator can change settings. FTB Quests is optional and needed only for quest mode; it does not submit tasks or claim rewards.

See [projector controls and image limits](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Towns-and-Kingdoms-Workshop#quest-projector).
